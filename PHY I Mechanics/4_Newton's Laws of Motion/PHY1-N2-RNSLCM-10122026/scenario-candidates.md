# Circular-motion scenario candidates

Four candidates for each scenario description. Pick two from each group (or mark cuts and replacements). Wind or current direction is not fixed here; left and right are applied later to groups 3 and 4.

Choosing two per group, then crossing groups 3 and 4 with both wind directions and all three positions, yields 36 items:

- Group 1: 2 scenarios × 3 positions = 6
- Group 2: 2 scenarios × 3 positions = 6
- Group 3: 2 scenarios × 2 wind directions × 3 positions = 12
- Group 4: 2 scenarios × 2 wind directions × 3 positions = 12

Radial equation used to filter these scenes, with the positive direction toward the center:

- Group 1: tension and weight. Top \(T + mg = ma_c\), bottom \(T - mg = ma_c\), side \(T = ma_c\).
- Group 2: normal force from a frictionless track, same pattern with \(N\) in place of \(T\). The object stays on the inside of the loop, so the track pushes toward the center.
- Group 3: horizontal tension, plus one horizontal wind or current. Same pattern with that force in place of weight. Direction is chosen later.
- Group 4: static friction on a flat, unbanked road, plus one horizontal wind. Friction takes the place of the normal force in the radial equation. The road's normal force is vertical. Direction is chosen later.

Drag, buoyancy, and a push along the path can exist in the story. The question will ask only for the radial component.

## 1. Vertical circle on a string

Tension, gravity, cord stays taut. 

comment: use 2 and 3, keep 1 and 4 as backup.

1. In a teaching lab, a rubber stopper tied to a cord is whirled in a vertical circle, and the cord stays taut. 
2. A gardener swings a small bucket of water, tied to a rope, through a full vertical circle, and the rope stays taut.
3. A student swings a steel nut tied to fishing line through a full vertical circle, and the line stays taut.
4. At a fairground game, a wooden ball tied to a cord is swung in a vertical circle, and the cord stays taut.

## 2. Vertical circle on a frictionless track

Normal force, gravity, object stays on the track. The track is smooth and frictionless. 

comment: use 2 and 3, keep 1 and 4 as backup

1. A roller-coaster car moves on the inside of a smooth, frictionless vertical loop and does not leave the track.
2. A toy car moves on the inside of a smooth, frictionless vertical loop and does not leave the track.
3. A glass marble moves on the inside of a smooth, frictionless vertical loop and does not leave the track.
4. An instrumented lab cart moves on the inside of a smooth, frictionless vertical circular track and does not leave the track.

## 3. Horizontal circle on a string, with wind or current

Tension is horizontal. One steady horizontal wind or current is present; its direction is chosen later. The string, not friction, keeps the object on the circle.

comment: use 1 and 2 for left to right force, and 3 and 4 for right to left force

1. A model airplane flies in a horizontal circle at the end of a horizontal tether in a steady horizontal wind.
2. A model boat moves in a horizontal circle, tied by a horizontal line to a fixed post, in a steady current.
3. A model car on a smooth, frictionless table moves in a horizontal circle, tied by a horizontal cord to a fixed peg, in a steady horizontal wind.
4. A small underwater probe moves in a horizontal circle at the end of a horizontal tether in a steady current.

## 4. Horizontal circle on a flat road, with friction and wind

Static friction is the inward force. The road is flat and unbanked. One steady horizontal wind is present; its direction is chosen later.

comment: use 1 and 2 for left to right force, and 3 and 4 for right to left force

1. A car drives at constant speed around a flat, unbanked circular track in a steady horizontal wind.
2. A delivery truck drives at constant speed around a flat, unbanked circular lot in a steady horizontal wind.
3. A bicycle and rider move at constant speed around a flat, unbanked circular path in a steady horizontal wind.
4. A motorcycle and rider move at constant speed around a flat, unbanked circular path in a steady horizontal wind.
